Introduction & Corporate Profile

African Wealth Empowerment Network (AWEN) (Pty) Ltd is a progressive, established South African construction and civil contracting business. Our solutions are project-tailored to suit client needs with strict attention to quality product, professional management, and sound technical advice.

"To become a leading and household name for institutional, municipal, and commercial clients in construction and infrastructure services."

From university campus renovations (UKZN Howard College, Mangosuthu University of Technology) to parastatal facilities (Transnet SOC Ltd) and provincial public works (Department of Health and Public Works KZN), AWEN delivers turnkey solutions built to South African National Standards (SANS 10400).
