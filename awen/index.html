Executive Summary

Progressive Construction & Engineering Solutions

African Wealth Empowerment Network (AWEN) (Pty) Ltd is an established, 100% Black-owned multidisciplinary construction and infrastructure contractor incorporated in 2014. We deliver turnkey civil engineering, structural concrete, commercial renovations, high-security perimeter installations, and institutional facility maintenance.

Our construction and consulting solutions are meticulously tailored to project requirements, driven by our foundational commitments to uncompromising quality, rigorous professional management, and sound engineering advice.

"We bridge technical precision with socioeconomic empowerment — creating resilient infrastructure that fuels South African communities and commerce."

From large-scale housing foundation slabs for Human Settlements to specialized high-traffic commercial renovations for Transnet, UKZN, and KZN Growth Fund, AWEN provides client-centered accountability at every stage of the project lifecycle.

Zero Harm Safety Philosophy

Health, Safety, and Environmental compliance are non-negotiable across every AWEN site. Guided by certified SACPCMP safety personnel, we strictly adhere to the Occupational Health and Safety Act (OHSA 85 of 1993) and Construction Regulations 2014. Our comprehensive Safety Management Systems protect our employees, subcontractors, clients, and surrounding communities.
