About Our Business

Established Contractor Delivering Practical Construction Solutions

African Wealth Empowerment Network (AWEN) (Pty) Ltd is a progressive construction company registered in 2014 (Reg: 2014/146827/07) and based in Woodlands, Durban. We provide general building, civil contracting, structural alterations, and facility maintenance services across KwaZulu-Natal.

Our construction and consulting solutions are tailored to suit each client's specific scope and budget. We focus on three core principles: quality workmanship, hands-on project management, and sound technical advice.

Our Vision & Mission

Vision: To become a trusted, household name for clients requiring dependable construction and civil works services.
Mission: To provide clients with professional, quality, and affordable construction services using experienced personnel and ongoing skills development.
