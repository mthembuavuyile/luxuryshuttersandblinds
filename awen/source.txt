1. Introduction
African Wealth Empowerment Network (AWEN) (Pty) Ltd is a progressive, established
business that provides various construction services.
Our Construction & Consulting solutions are project tailored to suit client needs, with attention
on quality product, professional management, and sound advice.
2. Vision
To become a leading and household name for various clients in Construction services.
